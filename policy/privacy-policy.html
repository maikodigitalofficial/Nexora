Privacy Policy

Last updated May 2026 Kisii, Kenya
Section 01

Overview

Welcome to SalonFlow Studio. We are a web design agency based in Kisii, Kenya, specialising in websites for barbershops, salons, and spas across Kenya.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website or contact us about our services. By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the terms of this policy.

We take your privacy seriously. We do not sell your personal data to anyone — ever.
Section 02

Information We Collect

We only collect information that is necessary to provide our services to you. This includes:

  • Your name and business name when you contact us
  • Your WhatsApp number or email address when you reach out
  • Your business location (city or town in Kenya)
  • Details about the website you want us to build
  • Photos or content you share with us for your website project
  • Payment confirmation details (M-Pesa or Pochi la Biashara transaction references)

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as ID numbers, bank account details, or passwords.

Section 03

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to deliver our services. Specifically we use it to:

  • Communicate with you about your website project via WhatsApp
  • Build and deliver your website as agreed
  • Process payments and send receipts
  • Provide ongoing support and maintenance if you are on a retainer plan
  • Send you updates about your project status
  • Improve how we serve our clients

We will never use your information for unsolicited marketing or spam. If we ever want to feature your business as a case study or testimonial, we will ask for your explicit permission first.

Section 04

Sharing Your Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share limited information only in the following situations:

  • With hosting providers (such as Vercel) to deploy and run your website
  • With domain registrars (such as Truehost or Sasahost) when purchasing a domain on your behalf
  • When required by Kenyan law or a valid legal process

Any third-party services we use are reputable providers with their own privacy protections. We share only the minimum information necessary.

Section 05

Data Storage & Security

Your project details and communication are stored in our WhatsApp conversations and any shared folders or documents we set up for your project.

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information including:

  • Keeping project files on password-protected devices
  • Using secure cloud storage for project assets
  • Not sharing your contact details or business information with anyone outside our team

We retain your project information for as long as we are working together and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you need support or want to continue services.

Section 06

Cookies

Our website (salonflowstudio.vercel.app) may use basic cookies to help the site function correctly. These are small files stored in your browser that help us understand how visitors use our site.

We do not use cookies to track you across other websites or serve you advertisements. If we add analytics tools such as Google Analytics in the future, we will update this policy to reflect that.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time.

Section 07

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask us what personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct any inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information when our work together is complete
  • Withdraw consent to us using your information at any time

To exercise any of these rights, simply reach out to us on WhatsApp at +254 713 324 672 and we will respond promptly.

Section 08

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites such as our social media profiles on Instagram, TikTok, or Twitter. Once you leave our website and visit these platforms, their own privacy policies apply.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of any third-party websites or platforms.

Section 09

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for business owners and adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 18.

If you believe a minor has submitted personal information to us, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

Section 10

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our services grow or as legal requirements change. When we make significant changes we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our services after any changes means you accept the updated policy.
