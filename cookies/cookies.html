What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (phone, tablet or computer) when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit — like which pages you viewed or how long you stayed.

Cookies do not give us access to your device and cannot read any personal files on it. They are widely used by websites across the internet.

We only use cookies to understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it. We do not use cookies to advertise to you or track you across other websites.
02

Cookies We Use

Nexora Studio uses two types of cookies on this website:

  • Essential cookies — set automatically by Vercel (our hosting provider) to make the website function correctly. You cannot opt out of these as the site needs them to run.
  • Analytics cookies — set by Google Analytics (GA4) to help us understand how visitors use our site. These are optional and you can opt out at any time.

We do not use advertising cookies, social media tracking cookies, or any cookies that track you across other websites.

03

Google Analytics (GA4)

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect anonymous data about how visitors use our website. This helps us understand things like:

  • How many people visit our site each day
  • Which pages are most popular
  • Which city or region visitors are from
  • How visitors found our site (Google search, Instagram, direct link)
  • Whether visitors are on mobile or desktop

Google Analytics does not tell us who you are personally. All data is anonymous and aggregated. Google may also collect and process data according to their own privacy policy which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on available at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

05

How to Control Cookies

You are in control of cookies on your device. Here is how to manage them:

  • Browser settings — most browsers let you block or delete cookies. Look in your browser's Settings or Privacy section
  • Google Analytics opt-out — install the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout
  • Mobile devices — check your phone's privacy settings to control app and browser cookies

Please note that blocking essential cookies may cause parts of our website to not work correctly.

Blocking analytics cookies will not affect your experience on our website — it only means we won't receive anonymous data about your visit.

Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes — such as adding advertising cookies — we will notify visitors with a notice on our website.
