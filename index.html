FAQ

Questions We Get Asked
All The Time

Everything you need to know before getting started. Still have questions? Chat with us on WhatsApp.

Will this actually help me get more bookings?

Yes, when the online journey is clear. Your site helps clients find you on Google, understand your services, trust your work, and message you on WhatsApp without confusion.

How soon can my salon be online?

Most Booking Growth Website projects launch in 2-4 weeks once your content, service details, photos, and booking requirements are ready.

If you need to move faster, we can map a staged launch during the strategy call: the most important booking pages first, then optional growth services after launch.

Do I own my domain?

Your domain is always 100% yours. We can guide domain setup, hosting, and launch configuration, and if you ever want to move, we help you do it with no drama.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Just your business name, logo if you have one, photos of your work, services, prices, location, and WhatsApp number. No logo? No problem. We will work with what you have and advise you on next steps.

Do you work with clients outside Kisii?

Yes. We serve salons, barbershops, spas, and wellness businesses across Kenya including Nairobi, Mombasa, Nakuru, Kisumu, Eldoret, Kisii, and beyond. Everything can be handled on WhatsApp.

What happens after my site goes live?

You can choose a monthly care plan for updates, support, speed checks, and Google visibility improvements. If you do not want a care plan, small updates can be billed only when you need them.

How do I pay?

We accept payment via M-Pesa and Pochi la Biashara. We take 50% upfront before we start and 50% on delivery when you approve the final site. Pochi la Biashara: 0713 324 672 (COLLINS KENYATTA)
