FAQ

Questions We Get Asked
All The Time

Everything you need to know before getting started. Still have questions? Chat with us on WhatsApp.

How long does it take to build my website?

It depends on your package. Starter takes 5 - 7 working days , Business takes 1 - 2 weeks and Premium takes 3 -5 weeks. Premium clients get daily WhatsApp updates and see a draft within the first 3 days.

Do I own my website after it's built?

Your domain is always 100% yours. Premium clients also receive the full source code. For Starter and Growth packages, we manage the hosting on your behalf — if you ever want to move, we'll help you do it with no drama.

What do I need to provide to get started?

Just your business name, logo if you have one, photos of your work and your WhatsApp number. No logo? No problem — we'll work with what you have and advise you on next steps.

Do you work with clients outside Kisii?

Yes — we serve clients across Kenya including Nairobi, Mombasa, Nakuru and Kisumu. Everything is handled remotely via WhatsApp so your location doesn't matter at all.

What happens after my website goes live?

You can choose one of our monthly retainer plans — Maintenance Plan at KES 5,000/mo or Growth Support at KES 15,000/mo. These cover hosting, security, updates and support. If you don't want a retainer, updates are billed separately at KES 2,000 per request.

How do I pay?

We accept payment via M-Pesa and Pochi la Biashara. We take 50% upfront before we start and 50% on delivery when you approve the final website. Pochi la Biashara: 0713 324 672 (COLLINS KENYATTA)
